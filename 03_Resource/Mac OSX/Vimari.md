
기본적으로 아래의 명령어를 동작시킬 수 있다.

### **In-page navigation**

```python
f       Toggle links
F       Toggle links (open link in new tab)
k       Scroll up
j       Scroll down
h       Scroll left
l       Scroll right
u       Scroll up half page
d       Scroll down half page
g g     Go to top of page
G       Go to bottom of page
g i     Go to first input
```

### **Page/Tab navigation**

```python
H       History back
L       History forward
r       Reload
w       Next tab
q       Previous tab
x       Close current tab
t       Open new tab
```
