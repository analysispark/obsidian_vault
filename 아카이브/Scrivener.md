Thank you for buying Scrivener. To activate your licence, please follow the steps below.  Note: If you bought using the in-app checkout, Scrivener may have been activated automatically. If so, you don’t need to do anything - just be sure to save this email somewhere safe in case you need to install Scrivener again in the future (for instance on another computer).  Downloading and Installing
	1	
	2	If you haven't already, download Scrivener by clicking the "Download" button above. Please ensure you are running our current version.
	3	
	4	Scrivener downloads as a .dmg (disk image) file. If this doesn't automatically open once the download is complete, locate the Scrivener.dmg file in your Downloads folder and double-click on it.
	5	
	6	Follow the instructions in the window that appears: drag the Scrivener icon into your Applications folder.
	7	
	8	Eject the Scrivener.dmg disk image in the Finder. You can now move the Scrivener.dmg file to the Trash if you wish.
	9	
	10	In the Finder, locate Scrivener in your Applications folder. Double-click to open Scrivener and start writing.
	11	
	12	If you wish to add Scrivener to your Dock, drag it from your Applications folder to the Dock (do not drag it from the install window to the Dock, only from the Applications folder).
	13	

Entering Your Licence
	1	
	2	When Scrivener launches, you will see a box telling you that you are in trial mode. Click on the "Enter License..." button.
	3	
	4	In the window that opens, enter your email address and the licence key displayed at the top of this email into the appropriate fields.
	5	
	6	Click the “Activate License” button. Within a few seconds Scrivener should be activated and the trial limitations removed. You’re now running the full version of Scrivener.
	7	

Finding Your Way Around
The best way of getting to know Scrivener is by going through the interactive tutorial available from its Help menu. It takes just over an hour to go through the section on the basics, after which you will know all of Scrivener's main features and be able to use it effectively for your writing projects. There are other sections in the tutorial to which you can return later when you’re ready to learn more.

We also have a number of video tutorials on our website, including a ten-minute introductory video that shows you everything you need to get up and running:

https://www.literatureandlatte.com/learn-and-support/video-tutorials?os=macOS
